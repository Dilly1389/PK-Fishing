👌 Welcome to PK Fishing

Here's how it works:

1. Build your rigs
On the Tackle Box tab, add your gear and build the rigs you fish. Tick "Use" on a rig to make it show up on your Timers.
2. Start a session
On the Timers tab, set your venue and how many rods you're on. Your rods appear, ready to go.
3. Run your timers
Start a timer when a rod goes out. Pick the rig, bait and bom you're using for that rod.
4. Log your catches
Got a run? Tap "Caught a fish", add the details and a photo. Your soak time is recorded automatically.
5. See what's working
Over time, My Insights on the Profile tab shows which rigs and baits actually land fish for you.

🎣 How the Tackle Box works

Private by default
Everything you add is only visible to you unless you choose to share it. Nobody sees your gear just by being in the app.
Share one item at a time
Hit "Share" on any item to give a specific mutual friend access — someone you follow who also follows you back.
Or share your whole box
The "Share my whole tackle box" card lets you give a friend standing access to everything — including anything you add later. No need to re-share item by item.
"Use" ticks what's actually yours
Something shared with you doesn't show up on Timers until you tick "Use" on it. That keeps your Rig, Bom and Hook pickers to just the gear you actually fish with — not everything anyone's ever shared.
Build rigs from your gear
Once you've added bait, dips and hooks, use them to build a rig further down. Tick "Use" on the rig too, so it shows up when you're logging on Timers.
Fill level for bait and dips
Bait and dip items get a slider showing how much you've got left — a quick way to see what's running low before your next trip.
